# Smart Bank Search v1.4 — Complete category reference

**141 built-in category groups**, including coverage for **all 24 OSRS skills** and activity-specific bank searches.
All searches use the normal RuneLite bank search field and show only items actually in your bank.

- `mining` — mining resources, tools, clothing and enhancements.
- `@mining gear` — just mining-related equipment, not all your ores.
- `fletching` — knives, logs, unfinished bows, feathers, shafts, bow strings, and more.
- `@fletching gear` — just the core fletching tools/boosts.
- `sailing` — candidate ship supplies and salvaging/repair equipment.
- `herb run` / `herblore run` — quick **herb-farming** kit (tools, herb seeds, compost, selected teleports).
- `herblore` — **potion-making** ingredients and tools. `herb` remains clean/grimy harvested herbs only.

Use the `@` prefix for *category-only* results; without `@`, RuneLite native-name and tag matches are preserved.
Combine TWO categories with `@ranged boots` or `@prayer legs`; the terms must be registered aliases.

**Accuracy note:** Equipment slots and stats are read from RuneLite where available. Most skill loadouts, Sailing and activity lists are manually curated matches by item name; this is a convenient *candidate filter*, not a complete or mandatory inventory checklist. Items missing from your bank cannot appear, and not every Sailing/new-game item is covered.

## Quick activity and gear-only searches

| Search | What it groups |
|---|---|
| `@herb run` | Herb seeds, secateurs, spade, compost/bucket, herb sack and patch teleports |
| `@herblore run` | Alias of herb run; **farming herbs**, not potion mixing |
| `@tree run` | Tree seeds/saplings, planting supplies and common transports |
| `@birdhouse run` | Birdhouse materials, clockworks, logs, seed and transport items |
| `@mining gear` | Pickaxes, prospector, Varrock armour, gloves, mining rings |
| `@fletching gear` | Knife and fletching boosts; use `@fletching` for all supplies |
| `@sailing repair` | Repair kits, planks, nails and boat repair tools |
| `@sailing salvage` | Salvage-related tools and supplies |

## Limitations and use

These are *search groups*, not optimized recommended inventories. For example, an herb run might use some of the available teleports rather than every one. You may store farming tools with tool leprechauns instead of in your bank. Search results are limited to items you own and have banked. Sailing rules should be revisited after future OSRS content releases.

The built-in game item search, RuneLite Bank Tags and numeric price filters remain available.
